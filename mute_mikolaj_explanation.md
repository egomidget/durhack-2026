# Mute Mikolaj

> *Selective active noise cancellation for the real world.*

## The Concept

Standard noise-canceling headphones block out everything, forcing you to disconnect from your entire environment just to avoid one distracting conversation. Mute Mikolaj is a real-time active noise-canceling concept built to selectively remove a single person's voice from your surroundings while leaving everything else audible.

## How It Works

Using `.wav` recordings of Mikolaj and other speakers, we trained a machine learning model to differentiate between voices in live conditions. By learning to recognize the distinct sound waves of Mikolaj speaking, the system picks his voice out of the surrounding audio to ignore and mute it on the fly.
